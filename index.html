Where Moss and Green Growth Show Up

Port Charlotte’s warm temperatures, frequent rain and tree-lined streets create ideal conditions for moss, algae and mildew. Shady roof slopes, north-facing walls and damp paver joints are the usual trouble spots.

Left untreated, growth can lift roof granules, stain finishes and make walkways slick. Early cleaning is usually simpler and gentler on your property than waiting until the buildup is thick.
